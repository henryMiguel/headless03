FRICTION DESIGN ARCHIVE ABOUT THE PROJECT

Cataloguing Friction
Design Strategies

A growing archive dedicated to the deliberate use of resistance in user interfaces. By cataloguing patterns that disrupt automatic habits, this project explores how intentional friction can be used to resist seamless design, provoke reflection, and reform our relationship with digital systems.
